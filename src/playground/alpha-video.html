Alpha clips

Clips carry a real alpha channel (VP9 WebM, composited by the browser) or stack colour over matte in one frame (H.264, recombined by a shader). The manifest says which, and the player picks the path. Switch the backdrop to check the matte: a correct clip shows no rectangle edge, no bright fringe on soft falloff, and no dark halo.
